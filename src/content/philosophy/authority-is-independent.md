---
title: Authority is independently enforced.
summary: The ability to act is granted separately from the plan that requests it, by a component that reasons do not control.
order: 40
---

A fingerprint or a compiled plan proves identity and lineage. It does not grant
capabilities or activate work. Admission binds an exact execution identity to a
trusted catalogue snapshot, resolved callable metadata, a host policy revision,
and finite grants.

Keeping authority independent means a clever plan cannot grant itself power, and
a compromised reasoning step cannot escalate beyond what was admitted.
