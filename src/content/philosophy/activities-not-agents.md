---
title: Activities can be agentic without agents becoming the architecture.
summary: An activity may contain a rich, model-driven loop. That does not make the agent the unit of scheduling, recovery, or authority.
order: 20
---

A single activity can wrap a coordinated model-and-tool turn that is as agentic
as the task needs. The workflow, however, remains the unit of durability,
recovery, and authority. This keeps a well-understood execution model around work
that is not itself well understood.

The point is not to forbid agents. It is to avoid making an unpredictable
participant responsible for guarantees the system still has to keep.
