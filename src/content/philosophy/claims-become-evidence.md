---
title: Claims become evidence.
summary: "Statements about reliability are backed by artifacts others can inspect: source, tests, CI, ledgers, and qualification results."
order: 50
---

It is easy to describe a system as robust. It is harder to make the supporting
artifacts visible. Penghou prefers durable records and reproducible checks over
confidence, and it is explicit about the boundaries of what has actually been
demonstrated.

Where something is limited, experimental, or unproven, the site should say so.
