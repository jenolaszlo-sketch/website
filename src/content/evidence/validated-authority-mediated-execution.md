The compile-and-admit boundary is described in the Fuwen repository: planning,
compilation, admission, and execution are separate, and admission binds the
exact fingerprint to a catalogue snapshot, capabilities, a host policy
revision, and finite grants. Hufu's three-platform CI and published-package
qualification record the current enforced boundary.
